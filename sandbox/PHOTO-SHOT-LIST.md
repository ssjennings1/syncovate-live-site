# Photo shot list — one photographer, one real session

Goal: five frames that show what it's like in the room, so the site stops being "a headshot and text."
Get a signed release from every participant before the session (one page, one line, one signature).

## Where
A real team session (Point the Finger or Hoops) at a client's place, or a DisruptHR green room + stage.
Natural light if at all possible. No conference-ballroom ceiling lights.

## The five frames
1. **The room, working.** Wide-ish, from a corner. Twelve people around a table or on their feet, Shannon in the middle, nobody looking at the camera. This one goes on Workshops & Retreats next to "Get your people in one room."
2. **Shannon at the wall.** Her, sticky notes or a flip chart, mid-sentence, hand up. Goes on Home under "Hard to see from the inside," or on How I Work.
3. **Hoops.** The hula hoop moment, people laughing. Goes on the Workshops page beside the Hoops card. Faces optional (backs and hands are fine).
4. **Two chairs.** Shannon and one owner at a small table, coffee, listening. Shot over the owner's shoulder so the owner is anonymous. This is the "coffee if you're local" image. Goes on How I Work next to the tiers.
5. **A clean portrait, same day.** Shannon alone, same light, same clothes, 4:5 crop, for Meet Dr. J and for the GBP / speaker one-sheet. Replaces the porch photo if it's better.

## Notes for the photographer
- Shannon is the subject in 2, 4 and 5; the room is the subject in 1 and 3.
- No stock-photo posing, no pointing at a laptop, no fake handshakes.
- Deliver 4:5 and 3:2 crops of each, plus the full frame. JPG, at least 2000px on the long edge.
- Brand colors on the site are charcoal, cream, warm gray, bronze and teal. A teal or bronze shirt on Shannon helps; black or white reads flat against the dark hero.
