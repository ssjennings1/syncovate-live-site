# Syncovate website: open-design brief for the page builder

Paste this first, in the same conversation, before any page prompt. This version gives you real design freedom inside the brand. The words are fixed. The design is yours.

## What we're building
A website for Syncovate LLC, run by Dr. Shannon S. Jennings ("Dr. J"), a business psychology consultant in Niles, Michigan. She works with founders and owner-operators of growing, privately held companies: trades, manufacturing, family businesses, professional services. Many are NOT MBA-trained. The site should feel like a trusted, plain-spoken advisor: warm, grounded, direct, human. Not startup, not corporate consulting.

## The one hard rule: the words
Use my copy EXACTLY, word for word. Do not rewrite, shorten, "improve," or add marketing copy. Do not invent testimonials, statistics, captions, labels, taglines, or claims. Every word visible on the page must come from the copy I give you (plus button and form labels in it). Decorative elements (shapes, lines, textures, icons without text) are welcome; anything that needs words is not, unless I wrote the words.

## Your job: make it visually memorable
The current site is too plain. Cream background, a headshot, and text. I want a page that feels designed and has a point of view, and still respects the brand. Make real design decisions: layout, rhythm, scale, how sections are shaped, how quotes and lists are presented, where color and dark bands appear. Surprise me within the brand.
Ideas you may use (your call): large type moments, an oversized pull quote, big numerals, asymmetric layouts, overlapping elements, a bold dark section, thin bronze line work, subtle geometric or architectural patterns (think blueprints, grids, lines connecting points), abstract texture in dark bands, cards with depth, a distinctive treatment for testimonials, scroll-triggered fades. Avoid: stock photos of people, handshakes, boardrooms, glossy gradients, neon, stars, clip-art icons, anything that looks like a template SaaS site.

## Brand (stay inside this)
- Colors: bronze #BF8756 (primary buttons, accents), bronze hover #A3703E, deep bronze #9E6D41 (italic emphasis on light backgrounds), teal #56ADBF (small accents only), deep charcoal #252830, charcoal #3D4148, cream #FAF8F5, warm gray #F0EDE8, white, body text #33363E. Do not introduce other brand colors.
- Fonts: Cormorant Garamond for headings, DM Sans for body. No other typefaces.
- Logo: use the Syncovate logo from the media library (search "SYNCOVATE WHITE&DARK"). Use it visibly: in the header, and large or as a subtle watermark where it helps. If it can't be placed, use the word SYNCOVATE in Cormorant Garamond, semibold, letter-spaced.
- Tone of imagery: honest, grounded, craft. Michigan trades and manufacturing feel. No people images unless I provide them.
