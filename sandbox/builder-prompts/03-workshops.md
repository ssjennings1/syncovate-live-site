### Team Sessions

## Team Sessions · from $1,500

Tried and true, ready to go. You bring the space and the snacks. I bring the rest. Ninety minutes each.

**Point the Finger** — When everyone's working hard and things still go sideways. Your people care. They have good values and they're trying to do the right thing. And somehow it still turns into missed handoffs and blame. This hands-on session lets the whole team feel that moment together. Then we talk about what's behind it: fuzzy expectations, crossed wires on who owns what, and the conversations nobody wants to have. Your team leaves with a shared way to talk about accountability and one thing to change on Monday.

**Hoops** — What does "good performance" mean here? Most teams are working hard toward a target nobody has quite put into words. This hands-on session starts with a challenge full of unknowns, the kind of goal you have to chase without a clear map. Then we use it to get on the same page: what good performance looks like here, how we'll know when we're hitting it, and how values and results fit together. Your team leaves with a shared definition they can work toward, and you leave with a clearer way to set expectations and have fair conversations about them.

**Your Go-To Power Move** — What's underneath the dynamics on your team, and how you learned it. Every leader has a default lever they reach for when the pressure spikes. Position, expertise, relationships, force of personality. In this session, leaders map where they learned theirs, usually long before they had a title, and then look at how it shows up with their team today. It gets real fast. People walk out knowing their own default, where it stops working, and one other lever to practice. Pairs with the Power Isn't a Dirty Word keynote.
