# Syncovate website: master brief for the page builder

Paste this first, in the same conversation, before any page prompt. Then paste one page prompt at a time.

## What we're building
A six-page website for Syncovate LLC, run by Dr. Shannon S. Jennings ("Dr. J"), a business psychology consultant in Niles, Michigan. She works with founders and owner-operators of growing, privately held companies: trades, manufacturing, family businesses, professional services. Many are NOT MBA-trained. The site must feel like a trusted, plain-spoken advisor: warm, grounded, direct. Not startup, not corporate consulting, not "boardroom handshake stock photo."

## Non-negotiable rule: the words
Use the copy I give you EXACTLY, word for word. Do not rewrite, shorten, "improve," or add marketing copy. Do not invent testimonials, statistics, logos, or claims. If a section needs text I haven't provided, leave a clearly marked placeholder instead of writing something. Keep headings as headings and buttons as buttons exactly as labeled.

## Look and feel
- Calm and confident. Lots of whitespace. Editorial, not busy. Think well-made printed magazine, not tech landing page.
- Colors (use only these): bronze #BF8756 (primary buttons, accents), bronze hover #A3703E, teal #56ADBF (small accents, labels), deep charcoal #252830 (nav, dark bands), charcoal #3D4148 (text on light, secondary dark), cream #FAF8F5 (page background), warm gray #F0EDE8 (alternate section bands), white for cards. Body text #33363E.
- Fonts: Cormorant Garamond for headings (medium weight; italic bronze (#9E6D41) for the emphasized phrase in a headline), DM Sans for body (light/regular, 17px, generous line height). Small uppercase-free labels in DM Sans 13px, medium, letter-spaced slightly.
- Sections alternate between cream and warm gray bands with 100px+ vertical padding on desktop (about 70px on mobile). Content width about 1120px; long text blocks about 680px wide.
- Buttons: rectangular, 2px radius. Primary = bronze fill, white text. Secondary = transparent with a charcoal 1px border. No gradients, no shadows beyond a very soft card shadow.
- Cards (pricing, offers): white, 1px light border, 32px padding. The featured card has a bronze border and a small bronze "Most Popular" tab.
- Testimonials: a thin bronze line on top, serif italic quote text (about 21px), then name in medium weight and role in small muted text below. No quote-mark icons, no star ratings, no avatar circles.
- Thin hairline rules (1px, 14% charcoal) separate list rows.
- Subtle fade-up on scroll is fine. No carousels, no auto-playing anything, no popups.

## Responsive and accessibility
