### Hard to see from the inside

## Hard to see from the inside.

The way decisions get made, the way your people talk to each other, the way work moves through the shop. All of it made sense when you built it. Then the company grew, and the things that held it together started getting in the way.

Here's what that looks like:

- Good people who aren't doing their best work.
- Meetings that end with a decision, and then nothing happens.
- A leadership team that agrees in the room and goes its own way after.
- Problems between your people that keep landing on your desk.
- Someone you love in a role they can't grow into, and everyone can feel it.

In twenty years, I've never walked into a company where the problem started with bad intentions. It almost always starts with good ones, and the habits that grew up quietly around them.

### The higher you climb

## The higher you climb, the fewer people tell you the truth.

Your team watches what you react to. Your partners and your family want certainty. Your peers are competitors. Somewhere along the way, you ran out of people who will say the hard thing, or ask the question that cracks the problem open.

That's where I come in. I bring twenty years in rooms with owners and their teams, a doctorate in business psychology, and a clinician's ear for what people aren't saying. I see what's going on underneath your results, and I know how to work with people through the hard parts, not around them.

Sometimes that's one session to unstick a decision that's been sitting too long. Sometimes it's a year of conversations that become the one place you don't have to have all the answers. Either way, you don't have to filter with me.
